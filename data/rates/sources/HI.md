# HI - sales tax rate sources

As of: **2026-10-01** (rates in force on the first day of 2026-Q4). Checked 2026-10-14.

## Sources

- Hawaii DOTAX: GET 4% + 0.5% county surcharge in Honolulu (through 2030), Kauai, Hawaii and Maui counties; none in Kalawao (directory rows, HI DOTAX). Avalara charges the same 4.5%. (The 4.712% pass-on maximum is not a tax rate.)
- Shape: 4% + county surcharge. Census 2020 ZCTA pieces map ZIPs to counties/cities.

## Validation against Avalara (Sales Invoices since 2026-07-01)

14 / 14 (state, ZIP, city) keys match (100.0%); 14 / 14 with the invoice's ZIP+4.
