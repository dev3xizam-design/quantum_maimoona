# MS - sales tax rate sources

As of: **2026-10-01** (rates in force on the first day of 2026-Q4). Checked 2026-10-14.

## Sources

- MS DOR: 7% general retail rate (rate code 74). City special taxes (e.g. Jackson 1%) are levied on sales made from a business location in the city, not on deliveries from outside sellers.
- Shape: 7% flat. Census 2020 ZCTA pieces map ZIPs to counties/cities.
