## Notes
- No local sales tax on general merchandise.
- Local option **meals excise** and **room occupancy excise** exist but apply only to restaurant meals and lodging. They do not apply to vape retail.
- Massachusetts' separate excise on electronic nicotine delivery systems is a different return.
