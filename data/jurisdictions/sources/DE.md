# Delaware (DE)

**Rows:** 1 (state: 1, combined_rate 0)  
**Code system:** Census FIPS `10` used only as a key — **Delaware has no sales or use tax and no sales tax return.**  
**Completeness:** Complete (nothing to collect).

## Sources
- Division of Revenue, Step 4 Gross Receipts Taxes: https://revenue.delaware.gov/business-tax-forms/doing-business-in-delaware/step-4-gross-receipts-taxes/ — "Delaware does not impose a state or local sales tax, but does impose a gross receipts tax on the seller".
- 30 Del. C. ch. 29, s.2905 (official Delaware Code Online): https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c029/index.html — retailer rate **0.7468%** of monthly aggregate gross receipts, less a $100,000 monthly deduction ($300,000 quarterly for quarterly filers).
- Gross Receipts Tax FAQs: https://revenue.delaware.gov/frequently-asked-questions/gross-receipts-tax-faqs/ — no GRT on goods the seller ships directly to customers outside DE.
- DoR license/rate list: https://revenuefiles.delaware.gov/docs/gr_rates.pdf (PDF dated 2016) — "Retailer - General" business code **396**, rate 0.007468.
- Raw copies: `raw/DE/`

## Preparer notes
- Quantum should never charge Delaware buyers sales tax. Remote sales into DE have no destination tax.
- Gross receipts tax is a license tax on the seller, not a tax passed to the customer. It applies only if Maximum Vapor is licensed or doing business in Delaware. It is filed monthly or quarterly on gross receipts above the exclusion.
- **Source conflict:** the DoR "Tax Tips for General Retailers" PDF (genretailers.pdf, last modified 2015) shows 0.7543%. The statute and the rate list both say 0.7468%. Use the statute.
- Vapor products may carry a separate Delaware tobacco/vapor excise. It was not researched here.
