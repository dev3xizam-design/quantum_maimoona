# Connecticut

**Status: COMPLETE.** 1 row: state only.

## Source
- CT DRS Sales Tax information: https://portal.ct.gov/drs/sales-tax/tax-information. It states: "The sales tax rate of 6.35% applies…" and "There are no additional sales taxes imposed by local jurisdictions in Connecticut."

## Rows
- **Connecticut (state)**: code 09 (Census state FIPS; CT uses no jurisdiction codes), 6.35%.

## Notes
- Returns are filed on Form OS-114.
- Other rates apply statewide by product type, not by jurisdiction: 7.35% meals, 7.75% luxury items (vehicles over $50k, jewelry over $5k, clothing/footwear/handbags over $1k), 1% computer/data processing, 2.99% vessels, 9.35% short-term car rental.
- Connecticut taxes electronic nicotine delivery systems under a separate excise, not the sales tax return.
