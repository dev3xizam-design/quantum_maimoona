# Oregon (OR) - jurisdiction codes

**File:** states/OR.csv - **1 row** (state)
**Code system:** none. There is no sales tax return.
**Status:** COMPLETE. Oregon has no general sales or use tax, so there are no state or local sales tax jurisdictions or codes.

## Source (official)
- Oregon DOR "Sales Tax in Oregon": https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/businesses/pages/sales-tax.aspx. It says: "Oregon doesn't have a general sales or use/transaction tax."

## Notes for the preparer
- **No sales tax filing:** there is no Oregon sales tax registration, return, or code for a remote seller.
- **Out of scope:** product excise taxes on vapor products and any city-administered lodging or prepared-food taxes are not general sales taxes. They are not in this dataset.
